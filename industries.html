01 Food & Beverage

FOOD
&
BEVERAGE

Bakeries, restaurants, quick-service brands and packaged food producers handle packaging every single day. It needs to hold up, look appetising on a counter, and go together fast when orders are moving quickly.

What The Industry Needs
  • Grease-resistant, food-appropriate board for fried and baked items
  • Fast, simple assembly for high-volume packing
  • Secure closure for takeaway and delivery
  • Presentation that looks good on a counter or in a bag
What Z&S Provides
  • Pizza Boxes & Burger Boxes
