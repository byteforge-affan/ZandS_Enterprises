1. Information Collected

We collect information you choose to give us directly, primarily through the contact form on this website. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any details you enter about your packaging or printing enquiry.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, or visitor-tracking scripts on this website. If that changes in future, this section will be updated to describe what is collected automatically (such as browser type or approximate location) and why.

2. How Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website is used to:

  • Respond to enquiries about products, pricing, and custom packaging requests
  • Follow up on quote requests and ongoing orders
  • Maintain records of business communication with customers and prospects

We do not sell, rent, or trade the information you submit to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Contact / Form Submissions

The contact form on this website is designed to send submissions using Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. At the time of writing, the live form endpoint has not yet been configured for production use — this section should be revisited once it is, so it accurately names the service processing your data and links to that service's own privacy policy.

4. Uploaded Files

If a future version of the contact form allows file uploads (for example, reference artwork or specification sheets), those files will be used only to process the related enquiry and will be handled with the same care as other submitted information. This website does not currently accept file uploads.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

This website's own code does not set cookies or store data in your browser's local storage. Third-party services embedded on some pages — such as the Google Maps location embed on our Contact page, Google Fonts, and (once configured) the Formspree form service — may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those directly if you have concerns.

6. Third-Party Services

This website currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for webfont delivery
  • Google Maps — to display our business location on the Contact page
  • Formspree — to process contact form submissions (pending final configuration)
  • Lucide — an open-source icon library, loaded as static assets with no data collection

These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service. We do not control, and are not responsible for, their individual privacy practices.
