1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Z&S Enterprises ("Z&S", "we", "us") handles information submitted through this website, zspackages.com. It covers the information collected through our Contact form — including uploaded artwork or project files — and how that information is used, shared, and retained. By submitting information through this website, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We collect information you choose to give us directly through the multi-step Contact form on this website. Depending on which fields you complete, this may include:

  • Your name, company name, email address, and phone number
  • The packaging category and box/structure type you're interested in
  • Quantity, dimensions, material, and printing preferences
  • Delivery location and required delivery date
  • Any project details or message you enter describing your enquiry

Files You Upload

The Contact form includes an optional "Upload Artwork / Design" field, which lets you voluntarily attach a reference file (such as a PDF, AI, PSD, CDR, PNG, JPG, or ZIP file, up to the size limit shown on the form) related to your packaging or printing enquiry. See Uploaded Artwork and Project Files below for more detail.

Automatically Collected Information

We do not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, or visitor-tracking scripts on this website. Some third-party services embedded in the site — such as the Contact form's submission service and the map on our Contact page — may automatically receive limited technical data (such as your IP address) as part of delivering their service; see Third-Party Services below.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website is used to:

  • Respond to enquiries about products, pricing, and custom packaging requests
  • Understand your packaging requirements, such as structure, material, and quantity
  • Prepare quotations and follow up on quote requests and ongoing orders
  • Review any uploaded artwork or project files relevant to your enquiry
  • Maintain records of business communication with customers and prospects

We do not sell, rent, or trade the information you submit to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information you submit may be shared with the service providers we use to operate this website and process Contact form submissions — see Third-Party Services below for the specific services currently in use. We do not maintain a broader list of third parties beyond those named on this page.

5. Contact / Form Submissions

The Contact form on this website is processed using Formspree, a third-party form submission service. When you submit the form, the information you entered (and any file you attach) is sent to Formspree and then delivered to Z&S Enterprises. We encourage you to review Formspree's own privacy policy for details on how it handles data in transit.

6. Uploaded Artwork and Project Files

The Contact form allows you to voluntarily upload artwork or project files (for example, reference designs or specification sheets) to help us understand your packaging or printing enquiry. Uploading a file is optional and is entirely your choice.

  • Uploaded files are used only to help us respond to the enquiry they were submitted with.
  • Please avoid uploading files that contain information unrelated to your enquiry, or more personal or sensitive information than is necessary.
  • We do not have a fixed, published retention or deletion schedule for uploaded files at this time; see Data Retention below.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

This website's own code does not set cookies, and it does not store data in your browser's local or session storage. Third-party services embedded on some pages — such as the Google Maps location embed on our Contact page, Google Fonts, the icon library we use, and the Formspree form service — may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those directly if you have concerns.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information submitted through this website. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of storage, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We have not obtained specific third-party security certifications for this website.

9. Third-Party Services

This website currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for webfont delivery
  • Google Maps — to display our business location on the Contact page
  • Formspree — to process and deliver Contact form submissions, including uploaded files
  • Lucide — an open-source icon library, loaded from a third-party content delivery network (unpkg.com) on some pages

These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service. We do not control, and are not responsible for, their individual privacy practices.
