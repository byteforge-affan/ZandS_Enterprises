01General

Z&S Enterprises designs, prints and manufactures corrugated and custom packaging — from protective shipping cartons to retail-ready, custom-printed boxes — for businesses across Karachi and beyond. Every order is planned around the product it needs to carry, from material and structure through to print and finish.

Related

Z&S Enterprises is based in Karachi, Pakistan. You can reach the team directly through the Contact page.

Structure, print and finishing are planned together as one discipline rather than treated as separate steps. Every order starts with the product it needs to carry — its weight, shape, how it ships and how it should look on arrival — not the nearest standard box.

Related
02Products

The range spans nine categories — Retail & Shopping Boxes, Food & Bakery Packaging, Pharmaceutical Packaging, E-Commerce Packaging, Toy & Kids Packaging, Gift Packaging, Jewellery & Luxury Packaging, Grocery & Consumer Packaging, and Custom & Promotional Packaging — alongside boxes, cartons, bags and labels. See the full range on Products.

Related

Yes. Alongside boxes and cartons, Z&S produces paper bags, labels, ribbons, stickers, tags and inserts — small components that complete a packaging system alongside a production run.

03Custom Packaging

Yes — custom dimensions mean structural sizing built around the exact product, not the nearest standard box. Outside the standard range, Z&S develops packaging to a client's own specification when it doesn't fit a catalogue.

Related

Custom orders can include structural sizing, offset or digital printed branding, consistent brand presentation across a packaging system, structural formats such as folding cartons, rigid-style presentation boxes and corrugated structures, and in-house finishing such as lamination and die-cutting.

Related
04Materials

Z&S works with corrugated fluting and board, selecting the right grade for the weight, protection and finish a product needs.

Every order starts with the product it needs to carry — its weight, shape, how it ships and how it should look on arrival. Structure, material and print are planned together around that requirement, not chosen separately.

Related
05Printing & Finishing

Z&S offers offset and digital printing, in single or multi-colour, for branding, artwork and product information on packaging.

Related

Finishing carried out in-house includes die-cutting, lamination and other finishing processes — planned together with structure and print for each order rather than added on afterward.

Related
06Our Process

Every order follows the same five stages: Understand — your product, quantities and how it needs to travel; Design — structure, material and print planned together; Produce — corrugation, printing and die-cutting carried out in sequence; Quality Check — every batch checked for structure, print accuracy and finish; and Deliver — finished packaging prepared and delivered, ready for use.

Related

Yes — material and print are checked at every stage of production, not only at the end, so results stay consistent from the first sample to a repeat order.
